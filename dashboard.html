A Python and SQL portfolio study of historical UCI Online Retail transactions.
Observed period: 1 December 2010 – 9 December 2011. All values in GBP.

Positive sales are recorded sale-line values after excluding cancellations and nonpositive quantities/prices. Returns are not subtracted; this is not net revenue or profit.

How do sales vary over time?

The largest complete month is 2011-11, at £1,509,496.33. December 2011 includes only the first nine days.

Where is the recorded sales value concentrated?

The United Kingdom accounts for 84.6% of the recorded positive sales value. This describes the data mix and does not explain its cause.

Repeat purchasing

65.6% of identified purchasing customers have more than one purchase invoice. Missing customer IDs are excluded from this denominator.

Read the results with these limits

  • Historical portfolio study, not current business performance.
  • December 2011 is partial; do not compare it directly with complete months.
  • Negative quantities, cancellations and nonpositive prices are excluded; returns are not subtracted.
  • Anonymous sales count in totals; customer analysis excludes missing CustomerID.
  • Exact duplicate-looking lines are flagged and retained because line identifiers are unavailable.
  • Shipping, charges and adjustment StockCodes are retained when positive; totals are not merchandise-only.
  • No observed trend is treated as a causal explanation or achieved business improvement.
